## FAQ

**Is goji berry good for the eyes?**
In classical food therapy, goji (枸杞) nourishes Liver blood — and the eyes
are the Liver's opening. Tang-dynasty texts through the Qing praise it for
"brightening the eyes"; modern research focuses on its zeaxanthin. Full
timeline of 8 classical texts: [goji berry page](https://www.liumeihua.com/ingredients/goji-berry/)

**What is the best food for bloating after meals?**
It depends on the pattern — food stagnation, qi stagnation, or cold in the
middle. Our bloating guide maps all three to classical formulas (Lingshu
Zhang Lun, Pi Wei Lun) with a 12-symptom self-check:
[bloating remedies](https://www.liumeihua.com/remedies/bloating/)

**How to sleep better with Chinese food therapy?**
The classic evening bowl is lotus seed + lily + millet congee; the rule of
thumb comes from Su Wen: "when the stomach is unsettled, sleep is restless"
— supper warm, small, early. Full guide:
[sleep remedies](https://www.liumeihua.com/remedies/insomnia/)

**Is ginger tea good for nausea?**
Ginger is the strongest evidence-backed kitchen food for nausea in the
classics ("the sage herb of vomiting") — with human trials supporting the
direction. Best in the morning, not at night.
[ginger page](https://www.liumeihua.com/ingredients/ginger/)

载，现代研究集中在玉米黄质。八部经典时间轴：
[枸杞页](https://www.liumeihua.com/zh/ingredients/goji-berry/)

**饭后腹胀吃什么好？**
先分型：食积、气滞还是中焦虚寒——腹胀指南把三型对应到经典方论
（灵枢·胀论、脾胃论），附 12 条症状自查：
[腹胀调理](https://www.liumeihua.com/zh/remedies/bloating/)

**失眠食疗吃什么？**
经典夜晚碗是莲子百合小米粥；总纲出自《素问》「胃不和则卧不安」
——晚饭宜温、宜少、宜早。完整指南：
[睡不安调理](https://www.liumeihua.com/zh/remedies/insomnia/)
